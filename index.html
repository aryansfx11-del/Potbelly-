Our Storyहमारी कहानी

पॉटबेली

Potbelly began in Delhi in 2011 with a stubborn idea: Bihari food deserves a proper table. In Patna, inside the Bihar Museum campus, we celebrate Bihar’s rich culinary and cultural heritage with a vintage, royal aesthetic.

Our menu travels through four lenses of Bihar — Mithila, Bhojpur, Champaran and Dehati — with a cozy, artsy ambience and house-made sattu coolers and Aam Panna.
